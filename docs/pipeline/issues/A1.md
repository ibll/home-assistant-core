## Why

Home Assistant has 2,000+ integrations, and testing all of them on every change takes hours. Every other stage uses this list to do only the work that matters.

## Tasks

- [ ] Replace the stub step in `team-changes.yml`. Diff against the merge base (`github.event.pull_request.base.sha` on PRs, `github.event.before` on push) and compute the four outputs that already exist.
- [ ] `integrations`: unique domains from `homeassistant/components/<domain>/` and `tests/components/<domain>/`, as a JSON list.
- [ ] `core-changed`: anything under `homeassistant/` outside `components/`.
- [ ] `requirements-changed`: `requirements*.txt`, `pyproject.toml`, or a `manifest.json` file.
- [ ] `workflows-changed`: `.github/**`.
- [ ] For ideas, look at the `info` job in `ci.yaml` and at `dorny/paths-filter` (pinned in the README). Don't copy all of it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A PR touching `homeassistant/components/demo/` outputs `["demo"]`.
- [ ] A docs-only PR outputs `[]` and `false` everywhere.
- [ ] The outputs are printed in the job summary.

## Artifacts

`changes.json` artifact with all four outputs.
